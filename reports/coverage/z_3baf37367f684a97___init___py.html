1"""Predicting battery electric vehicle energy consumption from EPA test data. 

2 

3MMA3001 Numerical Methods and Machine Learning, Monash University, 2026. 

4Individual project by Harshil Dobariya. 

5 

6The package predicts the energy consumption of a battery electric vehicle 

7(kWh/100 mi) on the EPA city (UDDS) and highway (HWFET) test cycles from its 

8design parameters, and compares a physics-based model with machine-learning 

9and hybrid models. 

10 

11Top-level inputs 

12---------------- 

13For each vehicle (all from the EPA test car data, converted to SI units): 

14 

15| Input | Column | Unit | Typical range in the data | 

16|---|---|---|---| 

17| Equivalent test mass | ``etw_kg`` | kg | 1,474 to 4,082 | 

18| Rated power | ``rated_hp`` | hp | 98 to 1,839 | 

19| Road-load coefficient A | ``f0_n`` | N | 74 to 295 | 

20| Road-load coefficient B | ``f1_n_per_mps`` | N/(m/s) | -2.6 to 13.7 | 

21| Road-load coefficient C | ``f2_n_per_mps2`` | N/(m/s)^2 | 0.22 to 0.82 | 

22| Drivetrain | ``drive`` | category | e.g. "All Wheel Drive" | 

23| Vehicle type | ``vehicle_type`` | category | "Car", "Truck", "Both" | 

24 

25plus the EPA UDDS and HWFET speed traces (s, mph). 

26 

27Top-level outputs 

28----------------- 

29Predicted unadjusted (laboratory) energy consumption on each cycle, in 

30kWh/100 mi, which converts to approximate window-sticker MPGe and, with a 

31known battery capacity, driving range (:mod:`ev_energy.validation`). 

32 

33Modules, in pipeline order 

34-------------------------- 

35* :mod:`ev_energy.data_cleaning`: load the EPA spreadsheets, fix mixed 

36 units, remove placeholders and window-sticker values, pair city and 

37 highway results (Stage 1). 

38* :mod:`ev_energy.physics_model`: road-load physics integrated over the 

39 drive cycles with finite differences and the trapezoidal or Simpson's rule; 

40 three-parameter least-squares fit (Stage 2). 

41* :mod:`ev_energy.splits`: train/test splits and cross-validation folds 

42 grouped by vehicle model, to prevent data leakage (Stage 3). 

43* :mod:`ev_energy.ml_models`: linear, decision-tree, SVR and neural-network 

44 models, and hybrids that learn the physics model's residual (Stage 3). 

45* :mod:`ev_energy.validation`: test-set evaluation, bootstrap intervals, 

46 input-domain check, sensitivity analysis and window-sticker conversions 

47 (Stage 4). 

48* :mod:`ev_energy.performance`: baseline and improved implementations of 

49 the physics kernel, timing, memory, FLOP and arithmetic-intensity tools 

50 (Stage 5). 

51 

52Each stage is run by a script in ``scripts/``; ``scripts/run_all.py`` runs 

53the whole pipeline. See the repository README for results and usage. 

54""" 

55
